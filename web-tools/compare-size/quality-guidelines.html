Quality Guidelines

Create your images according to these guidelines to ensure they're added to the tool!

4 STEPS TO A PERFECT UPLOAD
1

ONLY the Silhouette

Don't add a background or extra details apart from the silhouette. There should be no invisible objects and no background. All strokes and text objects must be converted to filled paths.

INCORRECT

Has excess details that aren't part of the silhouette

CORRECT

Clean silhouette

INCORRECT

Background is not transparent

CORRECT

No background

2

Get the Angle Right

All images must be from the side, front, or top of an object. For side and top views the object must be facing left.

INCORRECT

Object is not facing left

CORRECT

Object is facing left

3

Make a Good Silhouette

For a clean silhouette, the view must be perfectly perpendicular to the angle depicted, completely orthographic, and any pose clean and generic.

INCORRECT

Doesn't sit flat on the ground

CORRECT

Clean angle and pose

INCORRECT

Angle is not perfectly perpendicular and orthographic

CORRECT

Angle is perfectly perpendicular and orthographic

4

Keep It Simple

Create your images with just the right amount of detail. Too simple and it looks sloppy, too detailed and it takes a long time to load.

INCORRECT

Too simple (sloppy)

Too detailed (lags)

CORRECT

Simple but still clearly identifiable
